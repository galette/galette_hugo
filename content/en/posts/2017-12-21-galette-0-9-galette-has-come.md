<p>Months, even years of intensive developments; several candidates releases, a very hard work... And now Galette 0.9 begins!</p>


<p>Changelog for this release is quite long, I invite you to {{< doclink path="changelog.html#v090" >}}check the full changelog{{< /doclink >}} for details.</p>


<p>Even if changes are numerous for this release (which EOL 0.8 series), no database upgrade is required if you're runnig an up to date 0.8 :-)</p>


<p>Around this version, several services has been provided:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="http://forums.galette.eu/">Galette's forums</a>,</li>
<li><a href="https://vote.galette.eu/">a feedback platform for future features</a>,</li>
<li><a href="https://t.me/galettesoft">a Telegram group</a>,</li>
<li><a href="https://telemetry.galette.eu/">a Telemetry app</a>.</li>
</ul>


<p>And of course, all existing communication channels (<a href="{{ "/pages/contact/" | relLangURL }}">mailing lists</a>, <a href="{{ "/pages/contact/" | relLangURL }}">IRC channel</a>, <a href="https://bugs.galette.eu/projects/galette">tracker</a>, ...) are still available.</p>


<p>Please share your Telemetry informations and register as an user; this may help the project ;-)</p>


<p>I'd like to thank very much every one that has been involved in this release! And a very, very big thanks for those who tests candidate releases and have filled bugs that I have fixed.</p>


<p>I wish you all a merry Xmas and a very happy new year!</p>
